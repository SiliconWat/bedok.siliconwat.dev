# The Tipiṭaka specification has moved

The datasheets that were here — the `ed="K"` **Pāli witness** and the **Khmer translation corpus**
(roadmap **A64**) — now live in their own repository:

> **`SiliconWat/tipitaka-khmer`** (private) — `spec/datasheet-pali-witness.md`,
> `spec/datasheet-khmer-translation.md`, `spec/provenance-vri-cst.md`

They were drafted here only because the source PDFs are here. **The dataset is not the app**, and
holding the institution's most irreplaceable input's specification inside a reader's repo was a
placement of convenience, not a decision.

⚠️ **The PDFs under `tipitaka/` and `commentaries/` remain here and are unchanged**, but read the
spec before using any of them. Three things in particular:

1. ⛔ `commentaries/khmer/` is a **2009 first printing** — in copyright, blocked pending legal **L23**.
2. ⛔ `tipitaka/india/khmer/` is a **machine transliteration** of the Burmese text, not a Khmer witness.
3. ⛔ `tipitaka/japan/` is a **redacted derivative** of `tipitaka/5000/` — work from `5000/`, because
   the stripped pages carried the provenance.
